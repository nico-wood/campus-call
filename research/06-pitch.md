## 3. The 10 hardest judge questions

### 1. Is this gambling?

No. Entry is free. You do not buy virtual credits. They have no cash value, and there is nothing to cash out. You take a position, the crowd sets a market probability, and points score how well you forecast. That is a description of this product. It is not legal advice. A lawyer has not reviewed it.

### 2. Who is the user this weekend?

A person who opened the board this weekend and took a position on a public campus question. The proof is the app, not a story: {{live_forecasters}} forecasters, {{live_positions}} positions, including {{live_forecaster_handle}} on {{live_market_title}}. We will not invent users to fill a quiet board. Display handles only. No legal names.

### 3. Who pays?

Forecasters do not. The points are not for sale. The customer we would charge is a campus org that wants a standing board for questions it already posts in public. We have not set a price. We are not showing a logo, because nobody has agreed to be a customer. This weekend is evidence of use. It is not revenue.

### 4. What if the market is wrong?

Then it was a forecast, and it missed. A market probability is the crowd’s current read. It is not a promise. After the outcome, points move toward the people who were closer. One miss does not break the board. A board that never missed would be fake. We do not have a long record, so we will not quote a skill number. Resolved markets on the board: {{live_resolved_markets}}.

### 5. How do you resolve fairly?

The rule and the source are written before anyone takes a position. A human checks that source and confirms. The model may draft the rule. It cannot confirm the result. We do not open markets on a person, a grade, a confidential exam, or a result one person can set. If the source does not settle the written rule, we do not invent an outcome. What the app does next is {{resolution_if_source_unclear}}, and we will say only what the app actually does.

### 6. FERPA / student privacy

We do not make markets about a student, a grade, an exam, or an education record. We do not ask for a class roll or a student ID. The board keeps a display handle, positions, and points. A draft that names a person or a class record gets rejected by a human. This is the product boundary. It is not a claim that we are “FERPA compliant,” and the school has not been asked to approve it.

### 7. Why won’t this die on Monday?

It can, if the only questions were about this hackathon. The reason it should not: campus orgs already post public plans every week, and those posts keep making questions with real deadlines. The same draft step works on Monday’s announcement. We have no retention number. We will not invent one. We also have not booked the next org. The honest plan is one org, one real post, this same board.

### 8. What did AI actually do?

Only what we can show. It turned {{live_discord_source_label}} into a draft market: a question, a close time, and a resolution rule. It explained the move from {{live_move_before}} to {{live_move_after}} using the position log. The sentence on screen is {{live_ai_explanation_sentence}}. It did not forecast for the crowd, and it did not confirm {{live_resolve_market}}. A human did that, against {{live_resolve_source}}.

### 9. How is this different from Kalshi or Polymarket?

Those are real-money markets aimed at a national list of questions. Campus Call is a points board for public campus questions. Entry is free. Points have no cash value. We do not hold funds. The questions are local, drafted from a campus Discord post, and a human confirms them against a written source. We are not trying to be a national exchange. We are not giving a legal opinion about those companies.

### 10. What stops someone gaming it?

There is no cash to pull out. That removes the usual reason to cheat. We reject markets one person can decide. The rule is public, and a human confirms it against a named source, not against a vote of the forecasters. The position log is visible, so a pile-on is not hidden. A second account is still possible. We will not claim we have stopped that. Account checks that are actually shipped: {{account_check_status}}. A second account still earns only points.
