# Campus Call use cases

Research checked on Friday, Nov. 13, 2026. "This weekend" means a market that can close by Sunday night, Nov. 15. "Later" means it cannot close this weekend. Later ideas below still fall in fall term unless the entry says otherwise. Fall classes end Dec. 11, finals run Dec. 14–18, and the term ends Dec. 22.

Students take a position with virtual credits. The market probability is the crowd's current view. Accuracy awards points. Nothing here is a real-money stake.

Every question below is meant to close from a public page. If the named source never publishes the outcome, the market should void rather than be judged by rumor. No market below is about one student, one grade, a personal misfortune, exam content, or a call one person can make alone (a professor canceling one class is the pattern to refuse).

Two source conflicts are left as conflicts. Cal Poly's life page says campus dining has 800 menu options across 34 venues. Campus Dining's own site says 1,200 menu items and that 90% of orders are mobile orders. CIE's about page says Innovation Quest has awarded over $350,000. The Innovation Quest program page says more than $400,000. Do not average these.

### 1. Saturday volleyball vs Sacramento State
- Category: a
- Example question: Will Cal Poly win the Saturday, Nov. 14 women's volleyball match against Sacramento State at Mott Athletics Center?
- Resolution source: Final result on the Cal Poly schedule or the match recap at [gopoly.com volleyball schedule](https://gopoly.com/sports/womens-volleyball/schedule). The text schedule lists the match at home at 4:00 p.m., with no result posted when this page was checked: [text schedule](https://gopoly.com/sports/wvball/schedule/text). Conference context is the Big West standings, where Cal Poly was 13-1 in conference and Sacramento State was 9-5: [bigwest.org standings](https://bigwest.org/standings.aspx?path=wvball).
- Who cares: Students who go to Mott, club sports players, and anyone tracking whether Cal Poly stays ahead of Long Beach State for the top of the Big West.
- Risk flag: none for a team result. Do not open a side market on one player's stat line.
- Timing: this weekend.

### ASI
