APPENDIX · Q&A

Guardrails built into the pilot

Honest tokens

1,000 virtual tokens per person. Not redeemable, no cash-out, no purchase.

Verified resolution

Every market names its official source and rule before it opens. A human confirms every result.

Fair play

One account per person (email and phone). The team never takes positions. No market on a single private person.

Consent first

No organization is named as a beneficiary until it agrees in writing.

Compliance before scale

Athletics markets beyond the pilot wait for written sign-off from Athletics compliance. Cash features only through regulated partners.
