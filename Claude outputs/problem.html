THE PROBLEM

Local events create attention. Then it disappears.

For people

Following a game, a show or a charity event is passive. There’s no simple, social way to say “I called it” — or to see what the crowd thinks before it happens.

For hosts

Clubs, teams and nonprofits work hard to draw a crowd, but that crowd leaves no lasting connection and no new source of support.

For the community

Everyday local questions — the bus, the weather, tonight’s show — get argued in group chats. Nobody writes the number down.

Local events create attention, but most of that attention disappears when the event ends. People have no active way to participate, and hosts get no recurring connection or funding from the crowd they built.
