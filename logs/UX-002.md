## Resulting experience

- Compact wordmark/search/Portfolio/Settings header and horizontal categories replace the rail. Home opens on the cards, with a three-column desktop grid and one-column phone layout.
- Card titles link directly to their example. Outcome percentages link to that example's `#example-ticket`; native fragment focus and scroll bring the whole ticket into the phone viewport without adding a query parameter or client storage.
- Detail pages separate read-only probability data from the preview ticket. The ticket sits alongside content on desktop and follows the outcomes on mobile. The displayed probability bars use the unchanged example percentages; no historical movement is invented.
- Related examples cycle through the existing five examples, preserving the original category/search return context. Every example can appear in related navigation; there are no self-links.
- The backdrop uses an original static 128px SVG noise/grid mask with forest green and a small warm-gold glow. Cards, top navigation, ticket, and footer are opaque. Texture is reduced on phones and reduced further behind detail data. It adds no animation, JavaScript, or external dependency and ignores pointer input.
- Removed obsolete rail styling. Search and main content still share `.frame`; the list has no `.frame`/`margin:0` conflict.
